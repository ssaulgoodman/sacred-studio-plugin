# Festival sting

For occasions. The first 2 to 3 seconds are a greeting: the footage held blurred and dark, the greeting in the song's script landing one word per beat (scale down into place), the English beneath, then three flash cuts of moments from later in the Short on the last beats. The blur clears into the kinetic hook for the rest of the Short.

- **Inputs:** `--greeting` in the song's script and `--greeting-en`. Start the section 2 to 3 s before the first sung line.
- **Greetings:** Diwali `ಶುಭ ದೀಪಾವಳಿ` / `शुभ दीपावली` / `శుభ దీపావళి` / `தீபாவளி வாழ்த்துகள்`; Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi and others in the same form.
- **Everything after the sting:** exactly the kinetic hook.
