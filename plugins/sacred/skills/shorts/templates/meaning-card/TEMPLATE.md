# Meaning card

For lines whose meaning is the hook. Calm: the footage untouched, a slightly deeper grade. Each line appears small in its script with the reading beneath, then its English meaning arrives word by word, large, in Instrument Serif italic, centred in the lower third.

- **Best with:** devotional lines with a clear, quotable meaning; slower songs.
- **Keep meanings short.** Long meanings wrap to three lines and lose the effect; pick lines whose meaning fits in about eight words.
