# Kinetic hook

The default Short: the song's own footage full-bleed and untouched (no added zooms or punch-ins; the footage has its own camera moves), and the hook line set large at the bottom, each word rising out of a mask as it is sung. The romanised reading and the English meaning sit beneath it. The song title is a small label at the top.

- **Type:** the script in Tiro (Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Devanagari; Noto Serif for Malayalam) at 108 px; the reading in Instrument Sans; the meaning in Instrument Serif italic, warm cream.
- **Placement:** bottom-anchored 440 px up, clear of the Shorts interface; left aligned, clear of the right-hand buttons.
- **Grade:** a soft dark gradient under the lyrics and the title only. No borders, ornaments, glows, light leaks or particles.
- **Best with:** a chorus or refrain with 4 to 8 lines, footage with steady faces or hands.
